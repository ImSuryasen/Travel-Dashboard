Safety notes

A practical checklist for city walks, temple visits and the hill / lakeside days.

🏞️ Hills, fort & water

Wear shoes with grip; carry water and sun protection. Avoid slippery edges, restricted areas and entering the reservoir. Check weather and local access before Sinhagad, Khadakwasla or Panshet.

🚕 Getting around

Use known ride providers or agreed fares, confirm the vehicle before boarding, and share the trip / live location with someone you trust when travelling out of the city.

📱 Essentials

Keep phones charged, carry photo ID, a power bank and some cash. Save your flat address offline and keep a little buffer before the return train or flight.

🌙 Evenings

Stay on well-lit, active streets; use a trusted ride for the return to your flat. Check the last metro / bus timing rather than assuming service is still running.
